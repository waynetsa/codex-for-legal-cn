# v1.0.0: Controlled pilot-ready legal workflow template library for Chinese law firms

## Positioning

Codex for Legal CN is a legal AI workflow template library for Chinese law firms. It provides structured workflows, templates, references, evaluations, acceptance packages, audits, and controlled pilot materials.

All outputs are lawyer-review drafts only. This project does not provide legal advice.

## Included alpha-usable plugins

- `cn-commercial-legal`: commercial contract review
- `cn-corporate-legal`: corporate and M&A diligence
- `cn-litigation-legal`: litigation matter management
- `cn-ip-legal`: intellectual property workflows
- `cn-ai-governance-legal`: AI governance
- `cn-privacy-legal`: privacy and data compliance
- `cn-employment-legal`: employment and labor workflows
- `cn-regulatory-legal`: regulatory compliance workflows

## Main capabilities included in v1.0.0

- Skills for 8 legal workflow areas
- Editable templates
- Reference checklists
- Evaluation sample cases
- Fictional acceptance packages
- Regression records after alpha fixes
- Repository audit and v1 roadmap
- Controlled fictional pilot packages
- Safety and confidentiality documentation
- Private pilot, anonymization, and public/private deployment boundary guidance

## Not included

- Legal advice
- Real client data
- Real case materials
- Real contracts
- Real personal information
- Real regulatory materials
- Real MCP integration
- Real production system integration
- Production deployment

## Safety boundary

- Public repository materials must be fictional, clearly anonymized, or documentation/templates only.
- Real client materials must not enter the public repository.
- Real use requires authorization, private environment controls, access control, logging, audit, client isolation, matter isolation, and lawyer review.
- Outputs must not be sent, submitted, signed, published, or relied on without qualified lawyer or responsible-person review.

## Suitable use cases

- Internal legal workflow prototyping
- Training lawyers on structured AI-assisted drafting
- Building matter intake, review, checklist, and issue spotting drafts
- Running fictional or anonymized controlled pilots
- Preparing private deployment design

## Not suitable use cases

- Direct legal advice
- Direct external legal deliverables
- Production use with real client materials in the public repository
- Automated filing, submission, signing, or publication
- Unreviewed outputs
- Live connector use with real systems
